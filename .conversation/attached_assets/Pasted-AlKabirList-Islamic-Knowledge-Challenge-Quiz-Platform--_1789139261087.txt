AlKabirList – Islamic Knowledge Challenge & Quiz Platform

============================================================
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
============================================================

I have an existing website called AlKabirList.

Website:
https://www.alkabirlist.com

AlKabirList is primarily a community-focused website where community
announcements and useful community information can be posted and discovered.

I want to expand the website with a major interactive feature:

ISLAMIC KNOWLEDGE CHALLENGE

This should be a fun, educational, family-friendly quiz platform covering
the Qur'an, Islam, Seerah, Prophets, Islamic history, worship, manners,
and related Islamic knowledge.

The purpose is NOT to replace the existing community-list functionality.

The quiz system should become an additional engagement feature that gives
visitors a reason to return to AlKabirList daily and weekly.

The system must support:

- Children
- Students
- Teenagers
- Adults
- Families
- Islamic schools
- Community members

The application should eventually support daily quizzes, weekly challenges,
points, streaks, achievements, leaderboards, family participation,
school/community challenges, and additional game modes.

However, build the platform incrementally.

DO NOT attempt to implement every future feature at once.

10. RELIGIOUS CONTENT ACCURACY – CRITICAL
============================================================

Islamic religious accuracy is extremely important.

AI must NEVER automatically publish generated religious content.

ALL AI-generated questions must enter:

PENDING REVIEW

An administrator must approve the question before it becomes eligible
for public quizzes.

The system must NEVER silently change an approved religious question
using AI.

For Qur'an questions:

Include Surah and Ayah references whenever appropriate.

Example:

Qur'an 2:255

For Hadith questions:

Include source information whenever practical.

Example:

Sahih al-Bukhari
Sahih Muslim
etc.

Do not invent Qur'anic quotations, Hadith, scholarly opinions,
historical claims, or religious rulings.

If AI is uncertain about a religious claim, the generated question
should be flagged:

NEEDS VERIFICATION

rather than confidently presenting uncertain information.

Avoid unnecessary sectarian questions.

Avoid presenting disputed jurisprudential opinions as universally
accepted Islamic rules.

Questions involving differences of scholarly opinion should either:

1. be excluded from general quizzes, OR
2. clearly explain the context.

Accuracy is more important than producing a large number of questions.
