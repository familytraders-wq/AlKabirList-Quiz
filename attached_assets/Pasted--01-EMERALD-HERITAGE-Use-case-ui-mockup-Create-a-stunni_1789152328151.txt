### 01 / EMERALD HERITAGE

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a stunning, high-end website DESIGN PRESENTATION IMAGE for AlKabirList's "AlKabir Islamic Challenge", an Islamic knowledge quiz feature within an existing community platform. This is a static visual concept only. A professionally art-directed, sellable, mesmerizing design, sophisticated and highly usable for adults and families, not childish. Render a large sharp landscape board approximately 2400x1600 with a large flat front-facing desktop website mockup on the left (around 75% width) and a matching mobile quiz screen on the right (around 22% width). Clear readable typography, meticulous spacing and hierarchy, realistic UI. Small concept title outside screens at top. No perspective tilt, no overlapping screens. Desktop nav brand "AlKabirList" with Community, Islamic Challenge (active), About, Sign in. Community functionality remains visible in navigation. Feature eyebrow "ALKABIR ISLAMIC CHALLENGE"; hero headline "A little knowledge. A daily connection."; supporting text "Discover, learn, and grow together. One challenge at a time." Prominent daily challenge panel titled "Today's Islamic Challenge", "5 questions • About 2 minutes", primary button "Start Today's Challenge", quiet "Play as a guest". Audience selector labelled "Who is learning?" with K–2, Grades 3–5, Grades 6–8, Grades 9–12, Adult, Family. Below, tasteful category cards for Qur'an, Seerah, Prophets, Islamic History. Small bottom community invitation "Connected through community" with "Explore community listings" link. Don't overwhelm with future leaderboards or invented user counts. Mobile shows brand, "Today's challenge", "Question 1 of 5", progress 20%, question "What is the first Surah of the Qur'an?", four large answer cards "Al-Fatihah", "Al-Baqarah", "Al-Ikhlas", "An-Nas". No correct answer revealed, all options neutral. Large "Submit answer" button. Supporting "Take your time. Every question is a chance to learn." No fabricated scripture, Arabic pseudo-calligraphy, religious figures, people, or testimonials. Text is concise and immaculate. Geometric Islamic art may be used sparingly. Main focus is actual beautiful website UI, not marketing poster.
Concept title: 01 / EMERALD HERITAGE
Art direction: Luxurious emerald, pale warm ivory, restrained brushed gold. Elegant high contrast editorial serif display headings paired with crisp modern sans-serif labels. Broad ivory page with deep forest green hero visual and rich green CTA. Signature hero artwork: luminous sculptural Islamic arch in layered matte emerald stone, delicate brass eight-point geometric star lattice, golden sunlight through the arch; subtle depth, premium boutique cultural institution art direction. Hero copy left, sculpture right, daily challenge white card below integrated into clear grid. Rounded 16px cards, hairline borders, generous whitespace. Gold accents only decorative, readable dark text. Board background warm limestone. Timeless, prestigious, welcoming. Mobile uses ivory background and emerald controls.
